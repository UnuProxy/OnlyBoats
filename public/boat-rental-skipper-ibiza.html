No Boating Licence? No Problem.

In Ibiza, Spanish maritime law requires a licence to operate a boat without a skipper. That's exactly why every single boat rental at Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats includes a professional licensed captain — so you never need to worry about paperwork, safety, or navigation. You just enjoy the day.

FAQ

Boat Rental with Skipper — Questions

Do I need a licence for boat rental with skipper in Ibiza?

No. When you rent a boat with a skipper, no boating licence is required. The professional captain handles all navigation. You simply enjoy the day on the water.

Is a skipper always included in the price?

Yes. Every boat rental at Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats includes a professional licensed skipper in the price. There is no option without a captain — this is for your safety and to guarantee the best possible experience.

What does the skipper do during the charter?

The skipper navigates safely, recommends the best coves and beaches, handles anchoring and swimming stops, and ensures your group has the best possible day. They are local experts with years of experience on Ibizan waters.

How much does boat rental with skipper cost in Ibiza?

Boat rental with skipper in Ibiza starts from €762/day. The captain is included in this price — no extra charge. Larger boats range from €1,300 to €8,000+/day. All include skipper and fuel.

Can the skipper take us to Formentera?

Absolutely. A full-day boat trip with skipper from Ibiza to Formentera is our most popular charter. The crossing takes about 1 hour and the captain knows all the best spots on Formentera's coastline.
