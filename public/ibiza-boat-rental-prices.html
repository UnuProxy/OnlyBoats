FAQ

Ibiza Boat Rental Prices — Questions

How much does it cost to rent a boat in Ibiza in 2026?

Boat rental in Ibiza 2026 starts from €762/day for a speedboat with captain included. Mid-range motor yachts cost €1,300–€3,000/day. Luxury yachts range from €3,000–€8,000/day. Superyachts from €8,000/day. All prices include professional skipper and fuel.

Are Ibiza boat rental prices higher in July and August?

Yes. Peak season (July and August) prices are typically 25–40% higher than shoulder season. The best value months are May, June, September and October — the sea is warm and beautiful, but prices are much lower.

Is the captain included in the price?

Yes. At Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats, the professional licensed captain is always included in the quoted price. No hidden fees for the skipper. What you see is what you pay.

What is the cheapest boat rental in Ibiza?

The cheapest boat rental in Ibiza starts from €762/day for a small motor yacht (up to 9 guests). This includes captain, fuel and welcome drinks. For a 3-hour sunset cruise, prices start from €450.

What extras cost money on top of the base price?

The only paid extras are: food delivery (€30–€150), Seabobs (€150–€200/day), paddleboards (€50/day), event decorations (from €100), and premium drinks upgrades (from €80). There are no hidden mandatory fees.
