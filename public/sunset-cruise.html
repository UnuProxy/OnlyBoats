Ibiza's Most Iconic Hour — On Your Own Boat

Everyone talks about Ibiza's sunsets. The magazines, the influencers, the nightlife — they all reference that moment when the sky turns gold and the whole island stops. But there's a big difference between watching that sunset from a crowded beach bar and watching it from the bow of your own private yacht, anchored off Cala Comte with a cold drink in hand and no one else around you.

Our private sunset cruises in Ibiza depart at 6:00 PM from San Antonio Marina and cruise the west coast — the side of the island that faces the open Atlantic and catches every last ray of the setting sun. The route passes Es Vedrà, Ibiza's legendary rock island, then continues past Cala Comte and Cala Bassa before returning as the stars begin to appear.

Where We Go

Es Vedrà

Ibiza's mystical rock island. The light hits it in a way that's impossible to describe — you have to see it. We slow down here so you can take it in properly.

Cala Comte

The most photogenic beach in Ibiza. Layered turquoise water, perfect at golden hour. We anchor nearby as the sun drops to the horizon.

Cala Bassa

A sheltered bay 30 minutes from Ibiza Town. Crystal water, pine trees, and the beach club lit up as evening begins. A perfect final stop before heading back.

Perfect For Any Occasion

A sunset cruise in Ibiza is the most versatile charter we offer. Couples book it for anniversaries, proposals, and romantic evenings. Groups of friends book it as the perfect warm-up before a night out in Ibiza Town. Families book it on the last evening of their holiday, to see the island one final time from the sea.

We can arrange Champagne or Cava chilled and waiting on board, a flower arrangement for proposals, a charcuterie board, or pre-dinner canapés. Whatever the occasion — tell us when you book and we'll set it up properly.

What's Included

Every sunset cruise includes a licensed professional skipper, fuel, complementary drinks (cold beers, wine, soft drinks, water), ice, and towels. The duration is 3 hours. We also carry safety equipment as required by Spanish maritime law. No hidden fees — the price you see is the price you pay.

Can I Combine With a Full Day?

Yes — and it's a popular option. Start with a morning charter to Formentera or the hidden coves (6–8 hours), return to San Antonio Marina for a few hours break, then head out again at 6 PM for the sunset. Ask us about combined day packages when you book.

Questions

Sunset Cruise FAQ

What time does the sunset cruise depart?
Our sunset cruises depart at 6:00 PM from San Antonio Marina and return around 9:00 PM. In peak summer the sun sets around 9:15 PM, so golden hour falls perfectly during the cruise.
Where does the cruise go?
We cruise Ibiza's west coast — passing Es Vedrà, anchoring near Cala Comte, and passing Cala Bassa before returning. Your captain adjusts the route based on wind direction and conditions for the best experience.
How much does a sunset cruise cost in Ibiza?
From €450 per boat for 3 hours. For a couple that's €225 each — excellent value for one of Ibiza's most iconic experiences. Champagne and catering upgrades are available at extra cost.
Is the sunset cruise romantic for couples?
It's one of the most romantic experiences available in Ibiza. We can arrange Champagne, flower arrangements, or canapés on board. Several couples have proposed during our sunset cruises. Tell us what you're celebrating when you book.
What if the weather is bad?
Full refund if winds exceed 16 knots or there is rain. We monitor conditions daily and give advance notice. We can also move your date at no charge — we'd rather reschedule than give you a bad experience.
