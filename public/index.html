Boat Charter & Yacht Rental in Ibiza

Looking for the best boat charter in Ibiza? Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats offers 21 carefully selected vessels for every budget and group size — from intimate speedboats for couples to luxury superyachts for large parties. All yacht rentals in Ibiza depart from Marina Botafoch, the island's most prestigious marina, just minutes from Ibiza Town and the white island's most iconic spots.

Where Your Boat Charter Will Take You

Formentera by Boat

A 30-minute crossing reveals the Caribbean-blue waters of Illetes and Es Pujols. Most full-day charters anchor in the turquoise shallows for swimming and lunch on board.

Cala Bassa & Cala Comte

West coast beaches legendary for crystal-clear water and sunset views. Just 30 minutes north of Ibiza Town by boat, away from the road traffic.

Es Vedrà

The mystical rock island off the southwest coast — Ibiza's most photographed landmark. Perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and sunset cocktails at sea.

Cala Salada & Atlantis

Hidden coves accessible only by boat — perfect for guests who want to escape the crowds and discover Ibiza's wilder coastline.

Talamanca Bay

Close to Ibiza Town, ideal for shorter half-day charters or sunset cruises. Great for first-time boat charter guests.

Custom Itineraries

Every yacht charter is fully personalized. Tell our captain where you want to go — secret beach clubs, private bays, or fishing spots — and we'll build the day around you.

Boat Charter Options Available

Full Day Charter (8 hours)
Most popular. Departs Marina Botafoch 10:30 AM, returns 6:30 PM. Lunch stop at a Formentera beach club included on request.
Half Day (4 hours)
Morning or afternoon slot. Ideal for guests with limited time who still want the authentic Ibiza boat experience.
Sunset Cruise (3 hours)
Departs 6:00 PM. Perfect for couples, anniversaries, or pre-dinner drinks at sea with Ibiza's famous golden hour.
Overnight Charter
Available on select luxury yachts. Sleep in a cabin, wake up in a hidden bay. Princess V53, V65, V72, Mangusta 108, Azimut 60.

What's Included in Every Yacht Rental

Every boat rental from Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats includes a professional skipper, fuel for a standard 8-hour day, all safety equipment, fresh towels, snorkeling gear, ice, and a Bluetooth sound system. Most boats come with complimentary drinks. Premium yachts include water toys such as paddleboards, Seabobs, and inflatable platforms.

Booking your Ibiza yacht charter directly with us means no broker fees and no markup — you pay the boat owner's rate plus our small management fee. We respond within 15 minutes during business hours, and we know each boat in our fleet personally. Every captain has been with us for years and knows Ibiza's waters intimately.
