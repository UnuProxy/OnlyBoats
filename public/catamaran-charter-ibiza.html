Why Choose a Catamaran

Built for Groups, Made for Ibiza

The catamaran is the best choice when you need space, stability, and a flat deck everyone can enjoy.

Space for 11 Guests

Wide twin hulls create a massive deck. No one is squeezed in. Everyone has a sunbed, a seat, and room to move.

Flat & Stable

No heeling, no rolling. The wide beam means a steady platform even in open water — perfect if anyone in the group gets seasick.

Trampoline Nets

The mesh nets at the bow are a favourite — lie directly over the water, watch the sea pass beneath you. Kids and adults love it equally.

Best Price Per Head

For groups of 15–20, a catamaran works out cheaper per person than most private yachts. From €227/person for a full day.

Go Anywhere

Formentera, Es Vedrà, hidden coves, beach clubs — the catamaran reaches all the same destinations as any other boat.

Easy Swimming Access

Step down directly into the water from the swim platform. No long ladders, no hesitation — just a natural entry into the Mediterranean.

The Boat

Bali 5.4 Catamaran

Our Bali 5.4 is the most social catamaran you'll find in Ibiza. The entire cockpit is open-plan — no cabin blocking the view, no separation between guests. Everything flows from the wide stern platform to the trampoline nets at the bow.

  • Length: 17.5 metres (57 ft)
  • Max guests: 11 people
  • Cabins: 4 cabins + 4 bathrooms
  • Deck layout: Cockpit, flybridge, bow nets, stern platform
  • Swimming: Direct water access from stern
  • Shade: Full bimini covering entire cockpit
  • Navigation: Chartplotter, VHF, all safety equipment
  • Captain: Certified skipper included
