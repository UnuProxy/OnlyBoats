Ibiza Yacht Charter

Your Private Yacht, Your Rules

A yacht charter in Ibiza is the most liberating way to experience the island. No queuing for a beach bus. No fighting for a sunlounger. No crowds. Just you, your friends or family, a beautiful yacht, and a professional captain who knows exactly where to take you.

Unlike package boat trips, a private yacht charter means the boat is exclusively yours. You set the pace. You choose the coves. You decide when to swim, when to eat, when to head home. The captain is there to ensure your safety and share his knowledge — not to herd you with a group of strangers.

Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats offers 21 yachts ranging from sleek speedboats to magnificent 92-foot superyachts. Every charter is fully private, every captain is fully licensed, and every price is transparent — what you see is what you pay.

FAQ

Yacht Charter Ibiza — Common Questions

What is included in a yacht charter in Ibiza?

Every yacht charter includes: professional licensed captain, fuel, complementary drinks, snorkelling equipment, and local expertise. Optional extras include food delivery on board, Seabobs, paddleboards, and event decorations for special occasions.

How much does yacht charter in Ibiza cost?

Yacht charter in Ibiza starts from €762/day with captain and fuel included. Mid-range yachts cost €1,300–€3,000/day. Luxury yachts €3,000–€8,000/day. Superyachts from €8,000/day. Contact us for an exact quote for your dates.

Do I need a licence to charter a yacht in Ibiza?

No licence needed. All our charters include a professional licensed skipper. You just show up and enjoy — the captain takes care of everything.

How many people can charter a yacht in Ibiza?

Our yachts accommodate groups from 2 to 12 guests depending on the boat. With 21 boats in our fleet we can match any group size and budget.

Can I charter a yacht for a full day in Ibiza?

Yes. Full-day charters (8 hours) are our most popular option. We also offer half-day (4 hours) and sunset cruises (3 hours). Custom durations are available on request.
