Why Book Your Private Yacht Charter Directly With Us?

There are hundreds of boat charter brokers online claiming to offer the best prices in Ibiza. What most people don't know is that brokers add a commission of 15–25% on top of the owner's rate — so you're paying significantly more for the same boat, and the broker has never met the captain or set foot on the vessel.

At Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats, you book directly with the people who manage the fleet. Our founder Alin has been on Ibiza's water for 10 years, knows every captain personally, and has sailed on every boat we recommend. When you call or WhatsApp us, you're speaking to someone who will be at the marina when you arrive.

The Most Popular Private Yacht Routes

Full Day to Formentera (8 hours)

The classic. Depart 10:30 AM, cross to Formentera in 30 minutes (by speedboat) or 60 minutes (by catamaran or larger yacht), anchor at Illetes beach, swim, snorkel, optional beach club lunch, explore the south coast, return 6:30 PM.

West Coast Sunset Charter (3–4 hours)

Depart 5:30–6:00 PM. Cruise past Es Vedrà and Cala Comte at golden hour. Champagne and catering available. Perfect for couples, anniversaries, and celebrations.

Hidden Coves Day (4–8 hours)

Captains' choice based on wind and season. Cala Salada, Atlantis, Cala Xuclar, Cala Saona — the spots most tourists never find because there's no road.

Custom Overnight Charter

Available on Princess V53, V65, V72, Azimut 60, and Mangusta 108. Sleep aboard in a cove, wake up in Formentera. Contact us for multi-day pricing.

Private Yacht Charter FAQ

How much does a private yacht charter in Ibiza cost?
From €762/day for a small speedboat (all inclusive) to €20,000+/day for a superyacht. All prices are per boat — for a group of 8 on a mid-range yacht, this often works out to €150–250 per person per day, which is exceptional value for a full private yacht experience.
What is included in the charter price?
Every charter includes: professional skipper, fuel for a standard day, complementary drinks, fresh towels, snorkelling gear, Bluetooth sound system, and all safety equipment. No hidden fees.
Do I need a boat licence?
No. Every charter includes a licensed skipper — you need no experience, no licence, and no qualifications. You're a guest on board.
Is it cheaper to book directly or through a broker?
Booking directly is always cheaper. Brokers add 15–25% commission. When you book through us directly, you pay the owner's rate plus our small management fee — no markup. You also get faster, more personal service.
How far in advance should I book?
July and August: 2–3 weeks minimum. June and September: 1 week. May and October: often 2–3 days. Book as early as possible to secure your preferred boat.
Can I book a luxury superyacht in Ibiza?
Yes. Our fleet includes the Mangusta 108, Azimut 60, Princess V72, and other luxury vessels. These include en-suite cabins, full catering, and professional crew. Contact us for availability and pricing.
What is the cancellation policy?
Full refund if cancelled 15+ days before. Full refund for bad weather (winds above 17 knots or waves above 1m). We can also reschedule to another date at no charge.
