When Is the Best Time to Charter a Boat in Ibiza?

By Alin Letca · Updated May 2026 · 5 min read
Home › Guides › Best Time to Charter

July and August get all the attention — but ask anyone who works on Ibiza's water and they'll tell you the best time to charter a boat is often side-season. Here's an honest month-by-month guide so you can pick the day that suits you, whether you want peak buzz or warm, quiet water at a better price.

The quick answer

Our honest recommendation: June & September

If you want the best all-round day, choose June or September. The sea is genuinely warm, the sun is reliable, the famous anchorages aren't yet (or no longer) packed with boats — and the same yacht costs noticeably less than in July and August. You get the Ibiza everyone dreams of, without the peak-season crush or peak-season price.

May & October: the value play

The shoulder months are Ibiza's best-kept secret. The water is swimmable, the coastline is at its greenest, and you'll often have a cove almost to yourself. Prices are at their lowest, so it's the smart pick for groups watching the budget — see our cost guide for the numbers.

July & August: peak season

If you're coming for the full Ibiza energy — beach clubs in full swing, the island buzzing — peak summer delivers. Just two tips: book well in advance (the best boats sell out weeks ahead) and start early to beat the crowds to the best anchorages.

💡 Whatever month you choose, mornings are calmest on the water and the light is softest. An early start usually means flatter sea and emptier coves — then you're back in time for the evening.

What about the time of day?

Full-day charters typically run roughly 10:00–18:00. If you only want a few hours, a sunset cruise in the late afternoon is hard to beat — the west coast at golden hour is one of the Mediterranean's great sights.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to charter a boat in Ibiza?

June and September — warm water, reliable sun, calmer anchorages and better prices than peak July–August.

When is the cheapest time?

May, early June and late September: the same boats at noticeably lower prices, with the weather still warm.

Is May warm enough to swim?

Yes — the sea is around 19–20°C in May and very swimmable, warming through the summer.
