Why Go by Private Boat

Formentera is Best Reached by Sea

The ferry is crowded and drops you at a port. A private boat drops you at Ses Illetes — the most beautiful beach in Europe — and anchors right there while you swim in 10 metres of turquoise water.

You stop wherever you want. You swim at Espalmador — a tiny uninhabited island with a warm natural lagoon. You eat lunch on the boat. You come back when you feel like it.

That's the difference between a day trip and a memory that lasts forever.

Where We Anchor

Formentera's Best Spots

Ses Illetes

Voted the best beach in Europe multiple times. Shallow turquoise water, fine white sand, and the clearest visibility you've ever seen underwater.

Espalmador

A tiny uninhabited island with a warm natural lagoon. Only accessible by private boat — the ferry doesn't stop here.

Cala Saona

A sheltered cove on Formentera's west coast with dramatic pink rock faces and calm, protected water perfect for swimming.
