Why Take a Private Boat to Formentera Instead of the Ferry?

The ferry from Ibiza to Formentera drops you at the main port — La Savina. From there, most people rent a bike or a car to reach Illetes, the legendary beach on the north of the island. By the time you arrive, the best spots are already taken.

A private boat from Ibiza to Formentera is a completely different experience. You cross the channel in 30 minutes from Marina Botafoch, and your captain anchors directly at Illetes — in the water, metres from the shore. You step off into knee-deep turquoise sea. No taxi, no queue, no crowds.

"The private boat is the only way to do Formentera properly. Everything else is just a day trip."

You spend the morning anchored at Illetes, swimming and snorkelling. We carry drinks, fresh towels, ice, and snorkelling gear on board as standard. If you want lunch, we can arrange a table at Juan y Andrea or Es Ministro — both legendary beach clubs reached by dinghy directly from the boat.

In the afternoon, your captain can take you to explore the southern coast of Formentera — Migjorn, Cala Saona, or back towards Ibiza through the S'Espalmador nature reserve — before returning to Marina Botafoch by 6:30 PM.

The Day, Step by Step

1

10:30 AM — Board at Marina Botafoch

Meet your captain at Marina Botafoch. Cold drinks are already waiting. We go through the safety briefing and the plan for the day.

2

11:00 AM — Cross to Formentera

30 minutes at sea. On a calm day, the channel is flat and the views of both islands are spectacular. Dolphins are occasionally spotted on this crossing.

3

11:30 AM — Anchor at Illetes

The captain anchors in the turquoise shallows. You swim, snorkel, paddle, or simply float. This is what you came for.

4

2:00 PM — Lunch

On board (catered), or at a beach club by dinghy — Juan y Andrea is 5 minutes away and worth the reservation.

5

3:30 PM — Explore the south coast

Migjorn, Cala Saona, or S'Espalmador — your captain picks the best option based on wind and conditions.

6

6:30 PM — Return to Ibiza

Back at Marina Botafoch. The rest of the evening is yours — you're 10 minutes from Ibiza Town.

Common Questions

Everything About Formentera by Boat

How long does it take to get from Ibiza to Formentera by private boat?
From Marina Botafoch (Ibiza Town) the crossing takes approximately 30 minutes by speedboat. From Santa Eulalia marina it takes around 1 hour 15 minutes, and from San Antonio around 1 hour 40 minutes. We always recommend departing from Marina Botafoch for Formentera trips.
Is a private boat better than the ferry to Formentera?
Yes — the ferry drops you at the port, a taxi or bike ride from Illetes. A private boat anchors directly at the beach. You also have privacy, your own schedule, drinks on board, and you can stop at coves on the way back. The experience is incomparable.
How much does a private boat to Formentera cost from Ibiza?
Private charters start from €762 for a full day (8 hours), per boat — not per person. For a group of 8, that works out to around €95/person, including skipper, fuel, drinks, towels and snorkelling gear. Larger yachts range up to €5,000+ per day.
Can I have lunch in Formentera during the trip?
Yes. We can arrange a reservation at Juan y Andrea, Es Ministro, or other beach clubs — you arrive by dinghy and have a table waiting. Alternatively, we provide catered lunch on board. Let us know when booking.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, especially in July and August when boats fill 2–3 weeks ahead. We recommend at least one week's notice. In May, June and September, shorter notice is usually fine. WhatsApp us and we'll check availability immediately.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Full refund if winds exceed 16 knots or waves exceed 1 metre. We monitor conditions daily and give you advance notice if conditions look uncertain. We can also move your date at no charge.
