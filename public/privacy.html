Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website or contact us, why we collect it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish data-protection law.

Who we are

Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats is a boat and yacht charter business based at Marina Botafoch, Ibiza, Spain. For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@justenjoyibiza.com or by WhatsApp at +34 642 45 39 52.

What data we collect

We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry and arrange your charter:

  • Contact details you give us — your name, email address and phone/WhatsApp number when you fill in our enquiry form or message us.
  • Trip details — the dates, group size, budget and preferences you share so we can recommend the right boat.
  • Website usage data — basic, anonymous statistics (pages visited, device type, approximate region) collected through Google Analytics to help us improve the site.

We do not collect or store payment-card details on this website, and we never sell your data.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and send you boat options and prices.
  • To organise and deliver your charter if you book.
  • To improve our website and understand which content is useful (anonymous analytics).

Our legal basis is your consent (when you contact us) and our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that make the website and our replies work:

  • Usebasin / our form provider — processes the enquiry form so your message reaches our inbox.
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymous website statistics.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to message us there.
  • Our hosting provider (Netlify) — serves the website.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We do not share your data with advertisers or sell it to anyone.

Cookies & analytics

This website uses a small number of cookies, mainly for Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site. These do not identify you personally. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting booking, and then for a reasonable period for our records. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent or object to processing.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

To exercise any of these, just email info@justenjoyibiza.com — we'll respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
