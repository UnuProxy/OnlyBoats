Luxury Yacht Rental Ibiza — Questions

How much does luxury yacht rental in Ibiza cost?

Luxury yacht rental in Ibiza starts from €3,000/day for premium 50–65 ft motor yachts. Superyachts (65–92 ft) range from €5,000–€8,000/day. Ultra-luxury vessels like the Mangusta 108 and Riva Argo 90 are priced on request. All include captain and fuel.

What luxury yachts are available in Ibiza?

Our luxury fleet includes Riva Argo 90, Mangusta 108, Princess V65, Princess V72, Ferretti 920, Azimut 60, and Explorer 92. All come with a professional licensed captain included in the price.

Is a captain included with luxury yacht rental?

Yes. Every luxury yacht rental includes a professional licensed captain. No boating experience or licence needed. The captain handles navigation and ensures a VIP experience throughout.

Can I rent a superyacht in Ibiza for one day?

Yes. We offer single-day superyacht charters in Ibiza. Most luxury charters are 8 hours (full day). Half-day and custom durations are available. Contact us on WhatsApp for availability and pricing.

Total privacy, exclusive anchorages away from crowds, onboard comfort rivalling 5-star hotels, and a captain who is your personal guide to the island's most spectacular and secret spots.
