How Much Does a Boat Charter in Ibiza Cost? (2026 Guide)

By Alin Letca · Updated May 2026 · 6 min read
Home › Guides › Boat Charter Cost

It's the first question almost every guest asks us: how much does it actually cost to rent a boat in Ibiza? The honest answer is "it depends" — but that's not helpful when you're planning a trip. So here is a straight, no-nonsense breakdown of real 2026 prices, what's included, and where people overspend.

At Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats we own and operate our fleet directly — we're not brokers adding a markup — so these are the real numbers, not inflated quotes.

Ibiza boat charter prices at a glance (2026)

Prices are per day, for the whole boat (not per person), and already include a professional skipper, drinks, insurance and taxes. Fuel is the only major extra — more on that below.

💡 Value tip: A speedboat from €950 split between 8 friends is under €150 per person for the entire day — skipper included. That's less than a sunbed-and-lunch bill at many Ibiza beach clubs.

What's included in the price?

Every charter with us includes:

✓ A professional licensed skipper (so you don't need a licence)
✓ Drinks — water, soft drinks, beer and wine
✓ Fresh towels, a Bluetooth sound system, shade, a toilet and fresh water on board
✓ Insurance and all taxes
✓ Departure from Marina Botafoch or Marina San Antonio

Premium yachts also include water toys — paddleboards, Seabobs, inflatable platforms — and fresh fruit on board.

The one thing that's NOT included: fuel

This is where a lot of charter companies hide costs. We don't. Your price covers the boat and the skipper; fuel is billed separately based on the route you choose, because a relaxed day anchored near Ibiza Town burns a fraction of what a fast Formentera crossing does.

Before you book, we give you a transparent fuel estimate for your specific itinerary — so you know the all-in price upfront. As a rough guide, fuel for a typical day ranges from around €150 for a small boat close to shore to several hundred euros for a larger yacht doing longer distances.

How to get the best value

1. Travel in May, June or September

The exact same boat is significantly cheaper outside peak July–August — and the water is just as warm, with far fewer crowds at the best Formentera anchorages.

2. Fill the boat

Charter price is per boat, not per person. A 12-guest yacht split twelve ways is dramatically better value than the same boat with four people.

3. Book direct

Booking through us means no broker fees and no markup — you pay the owner's rate. Compare that against agency quotes and the difference is often 15–25%.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a boat in Ibiza for a day?

From around €950/day for a small speedboat up to €16,000+/day for a superyacht. Most groups spend €1,000–€3,000 for a comfortable mid-size yacht with a skipper, drinks and taxes included.

Is fuel included?

No — fuel is charged separately based on your route, with a transparent estimate given before you book. Everything else (boat, skipper, drinks, insurance, taxes) is included.

Do I need a boat licence?

No. Every charter includes a professional skipper, so you never need a licence or any boating experience.
