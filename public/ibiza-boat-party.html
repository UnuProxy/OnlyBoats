How to Organise the Perfect Ibiza Boat Party

A good boat party in Ibiza doesn't just happen. It takes the right boat, the right captain, the right setup — and someone who knows the waters. That's where we come in. We've done this hundreds of times and we know exactly what makes a group day exceptional versus just okay.

The first thing we do is understand your group. How many people? What's the occasion? What kind of music? Does anyone get seasick? Are there kids? All of this shapes our recommendation for the right boat, the right route, and the right setup.

Choosing the Right Boat for Your Party

For 8–12 people: Our catamaran is ideal — huge deck space, very stable, perfect for music and socialising. The Princess V53 is a great luxury option for groups who want cabin space and more shelter.
For 12–20 people: We have larger vessels available — contact us directly as these book out fast in peak season.
For 20–30+ people: Multiple boats sailing together, or our larger charter vessels. We've organised floating convoys for up to 50 guests across 4 boats — a truly spectacular experience.

Music & Atmosphere

Every boat has a Bluetooth sound system connected to the deck. For larger parties, we can arrange a professional DJ to join your charter on suitable vessels. Alternatively, we work with you on a playlist in advance so the music is sorted before you even board.

Food & Drinks

Complementary drinks (beer, wine, soft drinks, water) are included in every charter. For a party, we recommend upgrading to a full drinks package with Champagne, spirits, and fresh juices — pre-mixed cocktails can be arranged on request. For food, we work with catering partners who deliver directly to the boat before departure: charcuterie boards, sushi, BBQ, Mediterranean mezze — whatever your group prefers.

Decorations & Special Touches

Hen party decorations, birthday banners, balloon arrangements, floral setups, custom welcome messages on the boat, a Champagne toast waiting on board — we can arrange all of it. Tell us what you need and give us a few days' notice. We'll set up the boat before you arrive so it's a surprise from the moment you step on board.

Water Toys

A boat party is better with toys. We can add Seabobs (underwater scooters), paddleboards, inflatable platforms, water skis, and floating mats to any charter for an extra fee. These are especially popular on half-day party charters where the group wants to alternate between partying on deck and playing in the water.

Questions

Ibiza Boat Party FAQ

How do I organise a boat party in Ibiza?
WhatsApp us with your date, group size, and occasion. We'll recommend the right boat, sort out drinks, food, and extras, and handle everything so you just show up and celebrate.
What's the best boat for a hen party in Ibiza?
For hen parties we recommend a catamaran (up to 12, wide deck, very social) or a Princess V53/V65 for a luxury feel. Both have excellent sound systems and plenty of deck space. We can add decorations, Champagne welcome, and custom playlists.
Can I have a DJ on the boat?
Yes. We can arrange a professional DJ on larger vessels. Alternatively, the onboard Bluetooth system connects to any phone, and we can set up an extended speaker system for the whole deck.
How many people can come on a boat party in Ibiza?
Depending on the vessel, between 8 and 30+ guests. Catamarans and mid-range yachts handle 8–12. For larger groups we have bigger vessels, or we can coordinate multiple boats together.
How much does a private boat party in Ibiza cost?
From €762 for a small boat full day. For a catamaran or mid-range yacht suited to a party group, expect €1,500–€3,000/day all inclusive. Split among 8–12 people, that's very reasonable for an exclusive private charter.
