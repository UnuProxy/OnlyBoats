Why a Catamaran Is the Best Choice for Groups in Ibiza

When you're planning a boat day for a group — hen party, birthday, friends' trip, family holiday — the catamaran is the answer. No other boat type combines space, stability, social atmosphere, and versatility the way a catamaran does.

The wide twin-hull design gives you a deck that feels like a floating terrace. People can spread out, sunbathe on the front trampoline nets, gather at the back, or sit in the shade of the cockpit. On a motorboat, everyone is squeezed together. On a catamaran, everyone has space to move.

Catamaran Hire FAQ

How many people can a catamaran hold in Ibiza?
Our catamarans hold between 8 and 12 guests comfortably. The exact capacity depends on the model — we'll match you with the right catamaran for your group when you enquire.
Why choose a catamaran over a motorboat in Ibiza?
Catamarans offer a much wider, more stable deck — ideal for groups who want space to move around, sunbathe, and socialise. They're slower than motorboats (Formentera takes ~60 min vs ~30 min), but for most groups the extra space is worth it.
Can a catamaran go to Formentera from Ibiza?
Yes, absolutely. The crossing takes around 55–65 minutes. It's a beautiful sailing experience and very popular with groups who want the most relaxed, spacious day on the water.
Is a catamaran good for a group party in Ibiza?
Catamarans are the best boat type for group parties. The wide deck has plenty of space, music sounds great in the open air, and we can add food delivery, DJ setups, and decorations on request.
