Pricing & What's Included

How much does it cost to charter a yacht in Ibiza?
Yacht charter prices in Ibiza vary by size and year. 6 m boats start at €700/day all included (boat, skipper, fuel, taxes). Up to 10 m: €1,300 – €1,800/day. Up to 14 m: €1,800 – €3,000/day. 30 m and above: up to €20,000/day. We only work with fully licensed boats with proper marina moorings — always ask for a transparent all-inclusive quote before booking.
How much is the Shared Yacht Experience?
€200 per person, all included for 8 hours: boat, skipper, fuel, drinks (water, beer, soft drinks, wine), towels, snorkelling gear, ice and insurance. The yacht model varies based on number of guests — boats for 5 people are 8 m, boats for 11 people are 12 m — but the experience and inclusions are the same.
What is included in a private charter price?
The boat, the skipper, towels, drinks (water, beer, soft drinks, wine), ice, snorkelling gear and all taxes. Fuel is the only thing quoted separately — we give you a transparent fuel cost upfront based on your chosen itinerary. If you prefer, we can give you one final all-inclusive price with no add-ons and no surprises.
What is included in the Shared Yacht Experience price?
Everything. Boat, skipper, fuel, towels, drinks, ice, snorkelling gear, taxes and insurance — nothing extra to pay on the day unless you choose to add something.
What is NOT included in a private charter?
  • Fuel — quoted separately or bundled on request
  • Food — catering available via our partners
  • Extra hours — €100/hour (up to 14 m), €200/hour (over 14 m), €300/hour (over 20 m)
  • Mooring in Formentera port — most charters anchor in bays for free
Are there any hidden costs or extras?
No. We always send you the final all-in price upfront. What you see in the quote is what you pay.
Do you charge per person or per boat?
Private charter → per boat.
Shared Yacht Experience → per person (€200pp).
Does the price change with the season?
Yes. Most boats are priced in five tiers: May, June, July/August, September, October. A selected group of boats use a simpler structure — low season (May to 19 June), high season (20 June to 30 August), low again (1 September onwards).
Do you offer discounts for groups, kids or weekdays?
We don't usually publish discounts, but we make exceptions for selected boat models, weekday bookings or same-day last-minute deals. Always worth asking on WhatsApp.

Booking & Payment

How do I book a yacht with Just Enjoy Ibiza?
It's simple:
  1. Choose your boat (or pick the Shared Experience)
  2. We check live availability
  3. We send you a 50% deposit link (can be lowered to 30% in special cases)
  4. You pay the rest on the day of the charter
  5. You receive an official confirmation email immediately
Payments accepted: cash, card payment link, bank transfer.
How much deposit do I need to pay?
Standard deposit is 50%. In special cases we can lower it to 30%.
When do I pay the remaining balance?
On the day of the charter, the moment you board the boat.
What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, card via payment link, and bank transfer.
How far in advance should I book?
For the best boats and most popular dates (June–August weekends), book at least 1 month ahead. We also have last-minute and same-day availability — just message us.
Can I book last-minute or same day?
Yes. We usually need 1–2 hours' notice to prepare the boat and skipper.
Can I reserve a date without paying?
No — a deposit is always required to hold a date.
Do you take bookings on WhatsApp or only online?
We take bookings via WhatsApp — it's faster, more personal, and we can adapt better to what you need. Message us here.
Can I pay in cash on the day?
Yes — cash payment on the day is accepted.
Do I get a receipt or invoice?
Yes — always. By Spanish law all our charters come with a receipt or invoice. Just request the one you need.

Cancellations, Refunds & Weather

What is your cancellation policy?
  • More than 15 days before → full refund
  • Winds over 16 knots → full refund
  • Waves over 1 m → full refund
  • Rain → full refund
  • Cloudy or partly cloudy → NOT refunded (in Ibiza the sun usually returns within 1–2 hours, and the sea is usually calmer afterwards)
Alternative: we can move your charter to another day if there's availability.
Can I change the date if I can't make it?
With at least 15 days' notice — yes, subject to availability. If the new date isn't available we'll either offer you another boat or process a cancellation.
Who decides if the charter is cancelled due to weather?
The company decides, based on the professional recommendation of the captain.
What if one of my guests doesn't show up?
The charter still goes ahead. A private charter can only be cancelled with 15 days' notice.
Do you have travel insurance?
All our boats include insurance for guests during the charter.
What if I get seasick and want to come back early?
The boat is yours for 8 hours, so you decide when to head back. If you know you're prone to seasickness, we recommend taking a pill from any pharmacy 1 hour before the charter (always check with your own doctor first).

Shared Yacht Experience

What's the difference between Shared and Private charter?
Private charter → the whole boat is yours and your group's. You choose the itinerary, the stops, the timings, the departure and return time, the restaurant.

Shared charter → you buy individual seats on a pre-planned tour with fixed stops, itinerary and schedule.
How does the Shared Yacht Experience work?
We sell seats on a yacht for up to 11–12 people. When all 11 seats are sold, the booking closes. If we sell between 5 and 10 seats, a smaller yacht is used so the experience still goes ahead. You can buy as many seats as you want. It often becomes the highlight of someone's Ibiza trip — people make new friends on board most days.
Who will I be sharing the boat with?
It's a surprise — Ibiza style. We do our best to put compatible groups together based on the vibe of each booking.
How many people will be on the boat?
Maximum is 12 people (Ibiza law). Minimum for the experience to run is 5 people.
What if I don't want strangers on my boat?
Two options: book a private charter, or buy all the spots on a shared boat so it becomes private.
Can I book all the spots on a shared boat?
Yes — that turns it into a private charter at a fixed per-seat rate.
Are shared groups grouped by age, language or vibe?
Not by default. We try to match vibes when possible. If we're running 3–4 shared boats on the same day (40+ guests total) we deliberately match guests by age, language and energy.

The Boats

What kind of boats do you have?
Power boats from 6 m up to 33 m — from most major boat builders and designers, open style and classic style. Our fleet is updated every year with brand-new arrivals and older boats are refit to maintain a high standard.
How big are the yachts?
Private charter: 6 m to 33 m.
Shared Experience (11 guests): 12 m yacht.
Shared Experience (5 guests): 8 m yacht.
How many people fit on a boat?
The legal maximum in Ibiza is 12 people on any size boat. Smaller models are capped lower — 11, 10, or as few as 5 for 6 m boats.
Do all the boats have a skipper / captain?
Yes — every charter includes a professional skipper. For boats up to 10 m, you also have the option to rent without a skipper if you hold a valid boat licence.
Do I need a boat licence?
Only if you want to drive a boat up to 10 m yourself without a skipper. All other charters include a fully licensed professional skipper.
Are the boats licensed for tourist charter?
Yes. Every boat in our fleet has a commercial charter licence and is fully insured for guests.
Is there a toilet on board?
Yes — every boat has a private toilet on board.
Is there shade or a cabin?
All boats have shade. All boats over 8 m also have a cabin.
Is there a fresh-water shower on board?
Yes — every boat has fresh water on board.
Is there a Bluetooth sound system?
Yes — every boat has a Bluetooth speaker system, so you can play your own playlist.
Can I charge my phone on board?
Yes — all boats have phone-charging ports.
Is there Wi-Fi on board?
No — only a selected group of 30+ m yachts include Wi-Fi.

Routes & Itineraries

Where does the boat go from each marina?
From Marina Ibiza — 3 main routes: Formentera West, Formentera East, Ibiza South (up to Es Vedrá).

From Marina San Antonio — 2 routes; 99% of clients go to the West Coast.

From Marina Santa Eulalia — 2 routes; East Coast & North is the better option.

Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats mainly operates from Marina Ibiza (to Formentera or Es Vedrá) and from Marina San Antonio (West coast and sunset cruises).
What routes can I do from Marina Ibiza?
FORMENTERA WEST
• Classic (short, most fuel-friendly): Marina Ibiza → Cala Pluma (Salinas) → Es Palmador → Ses Illetes (lunch) → El Faro.
• Long: Adds Cala Saona and the Caves of Cala Saona.

FORMENTERA EAST
• Classic: Marina Ibiza → Es Palmador → Ses Illetes → Es Pujols (lunch) → the Cave → Cala en Baster.
• Long: Adds Es Caló.

IBIZA SOUTH
• Short: Marina Ibiza → Cala Pluma (Salinas) → Cap des Falcó → Cala Jondal.
• Long: Adds Es Torrent and Es Vedrá.
What routes can I do from Marina San Antonio?
Most charters here are 3.5-hour sessions (10:00–13:30, 14:00–17:30, 18:00–sunset). Full-day charters here are 7 h.

Normal route: Marina San Antonio → Cala Bassa → Cala Conta (2 anchor spots) → sometimes Cala Gracioneta → back.
Sunset route: Sunset in front of Café del Mar or Cala Conta.
Long route (extra fuel): Adds Cala Tarida and Es Vedrá.
Can we see Es Vedrá, Atlantis, Cala Bassa, Cala Conta or Salinas?
Yes — choose the itinerary that includes your preferred stop. See routes above.
Who decides the route — me or the skipper?
You decide, based on the skipper's recommendations regarding wind and waves on the day. Most of the time the wind blows east to west, so Formentera West and Ibiza South routes are the most reliable.
Can we stop at a beach club?
Yes — if it's on your route and offers dinghy service (a shuttle from the yacht to the shore).
Which beach clubs in Ibiza & Formentera have dinghy service?
Beso Beach (Formentera), Yamanja, Blue Marlin, Tropicana Beach Club, El Silencio, Cala Bassa.
Which restaurants in Formentera can we reach by dinghy?
West coast: Es Ministre, Juan & Andrea, El Pirata, Es Moli del Sal, El Tiburón, Beso Beach, Cala Dúo, Aigua, Can Carlitos, Sol (Cala Saona).
East coast: Tanga, Fandango, Chezz Gerdi, Es Caló.

These are the only restaurants reachable by boat in Formentera.
How long do we stay at each stop?
Anywhere between 30 minutes and 2 hours per stop. On a private charter you decide.
Can we visit multiple beaches in one day?
Yes — every itinerary includes multiple stops.
How long does it take to get to Formentera?
From Marina Ibiza: 45 minutes.
From Marina Santa Eulalia: 1 h 15 m.
From Marina San Antonio: 1 h 40 m.
Do you do sunset or sunrise cruises?
Sunset cruises — yes, departing from Marina San Antonio.
Sunrise cruises — no, we don't offer these.

Timings & Duration

How long is the charter?
Marina Ibiza & Santa Eulalia: 8 hours.
Marina San Antonio: 7 hours full day, or 3.5 hours half day.
What time do we depart?
For a day charter you choose your departure time from 10:00 onwards (8 h counted from then). The boat must return to the marina before sunset / before it gets dark, for safety reasons.
What time do we come back?
8 hours after departure, and never later than 21:00.
Is there a half-day option?
Marina Ibiza: only in May and September (4 hours).
Marina San Antonio: always, in fixed slots — 10:00–13:30, 14:00–17:30, 18:00–sunset.
Can the charter be extended on the day?
Yes — by paying the extra-hour rate. €100/hour (up to 14 m), €200/hour (over 14 m), €300/hour (over 20 m).
How long is the Shared Yacht Experience?
8 hours.

Is there parking at the marina?

Do you offer a transfer service?
Yes — Just Enjoy Ibiza Boats offers a transfer service in addition to the boat. Pricing depends on your location, starting at €50.
Can you pick me up from my hotel?
Yes — book the transfer add-on when you book the charter.
Can I come straight from the airport to the boat?
Yes — there's no better way to start a holiday in Ibiza than going straight from the airport to a day on the water.
Can I reach the marina by taxi or public transport?
Yes by taxi. No public transport line goes directly to the marinas.

Season & Weather

When is the best time of year to charter in Ibiza?
Between 20 May and 20 August the weather is typically sunshine every day.
What's the operating season?
15 April to 15 October.
Is the water warm enough to swim in May or October?
Yes — May is a little chilly but still swimmable. October stays warm after the summer heat.
What's the weather like in June, July, August, September?
Amazing — only a handful of rainy days across the whole season. September tends to have slightly more rain than other months.
Is the sea usually calm?
Yes — but not flat. There's almost always a half-metre swell, never more in normal conditions.
