The Experience

How the Evening Unfolds

01

Meet at the Marina

Your captain greets you at the dock with the boat ready. You bring drinks, music, and good company. We handle everything else.

02

Cruise the West Coast

We head west along Ibiza's coastline towards San Antonio — crystal coves, dramatic cliffs, and the sky already starting to glow.

03

Anchor at the Perfect Spot

Your captain anchors off Cala Comte or positions the boat in front of Café del Mar. Glass in hand, engines off — the best seat in Ibiza.

04

The Golden Hour

The sky shifts from blue to orange to deep crimson. You watch the sun disappear behind the horizon. No beach crowd. Just you and Ibiza.

Where We Go

Ibiza's Best Sunset Spots by Boat

Only reachable by boat — no tourists, no noise, just the sound of the sea.

Cala Comte
Multi-layered coves facing the open sea, with a wide unobstructed horizon. The water turns deep gold as the sun drops — one of the most beautiful natural settings in the Balearics.
Café del Mar · San Antonio
The most famous sunset in the world, watched from the water rather than a crowded terrace. Anchored in San Antonio Bay as the DJ plays and the sky turns crimson — unforgettable.

Common Questions

Sunset Charter FAQs

All sunset charters depart at 6:00 PM. This gives us time to cruise to Cala Conta or in front of Café del Mar in San Antonio and be perfectly positioned before the sun drops. The trip lasts 3 hours.
The two most iconic sunset spots for a boat charter are Cala Conta (stunning multi-level coves facing the open sea) and in front of Café del Mar in San Antonio Bay — the most famous sunset spot in the world. Your captain will position the boat perfectly based on conditions that day.
We can arrange a bottle of cava or sparkling wine for your group at an extra cost — just ask when you book. You're also welcome to bring your own drinks and food.
Our fleet ranges from intimate speedboats (7 guests) to luxury yachts (12 guests) and catamarans (11 guests). We have the perfect size for couples, friend groups and larger parties.
A sunset charter starts from €650 for a 7-metre boat and goes up to around €1,400 for a 12-metre boat. The price is for the whole boat — not per person — and always includes the captain, fuel and insurance.
